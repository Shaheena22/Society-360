- 🌐 **7 Indian Languages**: English, Kannada, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Gujarati.

- 🖨️ **Smart City QR Engine**: Live viewfinder scanner, department officer sticker generator, and printable grievance receipts.
- 👥 **Community Verification Counter**: Real-time 1 ➡️ 2 ➡️ 3 upvote increments.
- 🩸 **Emergency Hub**: Live urgent blood donor alerts and free health camp notifications.
